A Forestʼs Dream

An ancient forest dreams of Fiordland and the birds it shelters



In the last bastion of wilderness they call Fiordland, an ancient forest dreams. Told through the voices of Water, Rock and Tree — the elders who shaped this land across 85 million years — A Forest’s Dream traces the story of New Zealand’s extraordinary native birds: the white-water Whio, the flightless Kiwi and Takahē, the alpine Kea, and the forest-nesting Tawaki penguin. It is a tale of isolation and resilience, of the upheavals that arrived with humans and the predators in their wake, and of the fragile hope for a future where people and nature flourish together — a rambunctious garden.
